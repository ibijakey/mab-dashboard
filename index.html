Up 3,580 (+11.2% MTD) to 35,403, reversing September's decline and reaching 102% of annual budget. Liberia joins Rwanda, Cameroon and Ghana above target, the first Recovery segment market to do so.

PositivePortfolio
Fourth consecutive month of group growth
September closes at 2,474,420, +49,729 (+2.1%) vs August end, with 10 of 13 markets growing. The pace is slightly below August (+2.4%), and the close came in 8.0K under the MTD reading, mainly from Uganda and South Africa.
+49.7K MoM · 89% of budget
PositiveGhana · Zambia · Cameroon
Three markets deliver 84% of net adds
Ghana +19,553 (+6.4%) is the largest absolute gain, followed by Zambia +11,909 (+3.9%) and Cameroon +10,177 (+2.3%). Together they add 41.6K of the group's 49.7K, and all three close September at or near annual budget.
GH +19.6K · ZA +11.9K · CMR +10.2K
PositiveCongo-Brazzaville
Congo-Brazzaville +15.5%, second strong month
Up 6,837 to 50,962, following +22.4% in August. The market has added 14.9K since its July low of 36,057 and is now at 86% of budget, its best position of the year. Root cause should be confirmed before it is called a structural recovery.
+6.8K MoM · 86% of budget

Uganda the largest drag at month end
Down 16,238 (−7.3%) from August's 221,255 to 205,017, and a further 8.4K below the September MTD reading. Uganda has rebounded strongly in October MTD (231,875), so the September dip needs explaining before either move is treated as a trend.
